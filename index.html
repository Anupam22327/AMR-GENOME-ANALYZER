Core Bioinformatics Capabilities

Built specifically for biotechnology education and genomic resistance analysis

🛡️

1. AMR Gene Detection

Identifies clinically relevant antimicrobial resistance determinants including beta-lactamases (blaCTX-M, blaTEM), methicillin resistance (mecA), tetracycline pumps (tetA), and vancomycin resistance operon (vanA) with exact genomic bounds, identity scores, and mechanisms.

Explore Genes →
⚡

2. Mutation Analysis

Deep-dives into single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs), missense mutations, nonsense truncations, and frameshift indels. Translates nucleotide changes into codon and amino acid substitutions (e.g. GyrA Ser83Leu, PBP2a Val274Ile) with verified clinical evidence tiers.

Analyze Mutations →
🧬

3. Genome Visualization

Dynamically plots genomic coordinates across contig tracks with exact mutation pointer needles, locus zoom windows, and interactive 3D molecular protein structure visualization powered by 3Dmol.js to observe catalytic pocket alterations in real-time.

View Visualizations →
⚠️

IMPORTANT BIOINFORMATICS & ACADEMIC DISCLAIMER

This application is an educational bioinformatics demonstration. Results generated using demonstration or reference data must NOT be interpreted as clinical diagnosis, treatment guidance, or validated antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST).

Validated AMR analysis in production environments requires curated reference databases (such as CARD, NCBI AMRFinderPlus, ResFinder) and validated alignment pipelines (BLAST+, Diamond, or HMMER).
