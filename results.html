Primary Antimicrobial Resistance Mechanisms

1. Drug Inactivation

Bacterial enzymes hydrolyze or chemically modify antibiotics before they reach targets.

blaCTX-M-15 • blaTEM-1
Hydrolysis of beta-lactam ring

2. Target Modification

Enzymatic mutation or methylation lowers drug affinity while preserving biological function.

mecA (PBP2a) • gyrA (Ser83Leu)
ermB (23S rRNA) • vanA (D-Ala-D-Lac)

3. Efflux & Target Protection

Active extrusion pumps out antibiotic molecules; shield proteins physically prevent binding.

tetA (MFS Proton Antiporter)
qnrS1 (Topoisomerase Protection)
